A Tradition of Excellence

Universidade Gregório Semedo (UGS) stands as a beacon of higher learning in Angola, dedicated to fostering intellectual growth and professional development. Established with a vision to transform the educational landscape, UGS has consistently evolved to meet the demands of a dynamic global society.

Our institution prides itself on a rigorous academic curriculum, distinguished faculty, and a commitment to research that addresses real-world challenges. We believe that education extends beyond the classroom, cultivating critical thinkers and ethical leaders ready to contribute meaningfully to their communities.

Our History Timeline

2004 Foundation

Universidade Gregório Semedo was officially founded, starting with three faculties and a vision to change the educational paradigm in Luanda.

2010 Campus Expansion

Inauguration of the new main campus building, featuring modern laboratories, a vast library, and increased classroom capacity to accommodate growing student numbers.

2016 International Accreditation

UGS achieved key international partnerships and accreditation for its Business and Law programs, setting a new standard for private education quality.

2023 Digital Transformation

Launch of the fully integrated digital student portal and hybrid learning classrooms, modernizing the entire academic experience.
