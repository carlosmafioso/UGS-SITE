Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

When are the admissions deadlines?

expand_more

Admissions for the 2024/25 academic year are currently open. The priority deadline is August 31st, but we accept applications on a rolling basis until classes are full.

Does UGS offer student housing?

expand_more

While we do not have on-campus dormitories, the Student Welfare office maintains a curated list of trusted private accommodations and student apartments near the campus.

How can I schedule a campus tour?

expand_more

You can schedule a guided tour by emailing admissions@ugs.ed.ao or using the "Virtual Tour" feature on our homepage. In-person tours are available Monday through Friday at 10 AM and 2 PM.

Are there scholarship opportunities?

expand_more

Yes, UGS offers several merit-based scholarships and financial aid programs. Please visit the Admissions office or check our Student Portal for eligibility criteria and application forms.

Need Immediate Assistance?

Our student support team is available online to chat with you in real-time during business hours.
