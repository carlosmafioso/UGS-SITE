About the Course

The Law Degree at Universidade Gregório Semedo is designed to form legal professionals capable of interpreting and applying the law with a high sense of justice and social responsibility. Our program combines rigorous theoretical foundations with practical case studies, ensuring graduates are well-equipped for the complexities of modern legal systems.

Students will explore various branches of law including Civil, Criminal, Constitutional, and International Law. The curriculum is constantly updated to reflect current legislative changes and global legal trends, fostering critical thinking and argumentative skills essential for success in the field.
